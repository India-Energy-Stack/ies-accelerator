A D G V C L consumer, recorded by the DISCOM.

"Earlier, whenever I needed address proof, I would search for my electricity bill, and sometimes it was lost or torn. Now, with the D G V C L Consumer Energy Passport, my verified address is always on my phone. I can show it to a bank or any other stakeholder, and it is accepted in a minute. This is a truly digital identity. No running around anymore."

What the consumer noticed is exactly the Passport's design intent: a verified record they hold themselves, accepted without a visit or a photocopy.

D G V C L took it to the public with its own campaign — a state-wide portal at passport dot g u v n l dot com, and the one nine one two three helpline, inviting every consumer to get theirs.
