One proposed initiative, and it can start next week.

Pledge one: "We will be I E S Ready." An organisation commits to implement the specifications and certify for at least one use case. That is open to utilities, A M I S Ps, technology providers, banks and lenders.

Pledge two: "We will contribute." An organisation commits to develop and review schemas in the open, on the I E S GitHub. That is how the standard grows beyond the founding team.

Government bodies are invited to pledge first — N E G D, M N R E and others alongside utilities and providers, so that public institutions lead by example. I D A has offered to help carry the pledge to more DISCOMs.

A pledge converts interest into named commitment. When governance stands up, it inherits a public register of organisations already committed to implement, and to contribute.
