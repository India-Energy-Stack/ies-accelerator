Arun Kumar, a farmer and prosumer in Meerut, was one of the first consumers in the peer-to-peer energy-trading pilot. In a recent message to the I E S team, he wrote:

"For the past six months I have been working without pause to make people aware of solar energy and peer-to-peer energy trading. So far I have taken this message to sixty-two villages, through more than a hundred videos. My aim is not personal gain, but to inspire people at the grassroots to adopt solar energy and understand peer-to-peer energy trading."

Sixty-two villages. More than a hundred awareness videos. Six months of campaigning, unprompted.

The peer-to-peer use case was launched at the A I Impact Summit in February with B R P L, T P D D L and P V V N L. Six months on, one of its first consumers is carrying the idea from village to village on his own initiative. Demand is forming at the grassroots ahead of the national rollout, and the people who tried it first are becoming its advocates.
