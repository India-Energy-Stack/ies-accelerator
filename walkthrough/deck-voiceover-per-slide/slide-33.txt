I E S has been seen at the highest level twice in two months.

At the A I Impact Summit in February, the energy-trading use case with B R P L, T P D D L and P V V N L was launched in the presence of the Honourable Prime Minister, and four ministers visited the I E S pavilion. At the Bharat Electricity Summit in March, Energy Credentials were issued live through DigiLocker to a national audience, with N E G D. And the pavilion itself ran across four booths with R E C, MeitY and the International Solar Alliance.

The recognition matters, but it is not the outcome. The outcome is six utilities running live use cases on a published specification.
