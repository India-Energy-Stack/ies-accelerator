Everything I E S does reduces to three steps.

Register. Every participant, every meter, every solar unit gets a verified digital identity in a shared registry. Created once, recognised everywhere, with no repeat K Y C. Identities are revalidated on a set cycle and can be revoked, so the records stay current as meters and solar units change.

Discover. Before any data moves, the two parties find each other in the registry and check credentials automatically. Who are you, what may you ask for, and on whose consent.

Exchange. Data then moves on published schemas built on a uniform taxonomy. Every exchange is consented, signed and receipted, so what was shared, with whom, and why is a matter of record.

Three steps, every use case, every participant. Whether the data moves inline or in bulk is a single decision made at contract time.
