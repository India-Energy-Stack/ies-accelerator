The original order envisaged a single proof of concept. What happened was more interesting, and it split into two paths.

Four utilities went all in. Tata Power, A P E P D C L, D G V C L and P V V N L each did the full build: their own adapter, their own keys, multiple use cases on their own systems. That is the deep path, and it took thirty days per utility.

Three came for one use case. B R P L, T P D D L and P V V N L joined for a single use case, demonstrated live at the A I Impact Summit — one use case, with no full onboarding first.

One pilot in the original outlook became four full implementations plus three single use-case utilities: six unique utilities live today.

The lesson for the next cohort is that a utility can onboard whole, or start with the single use case it needs most. The standard carries both.
