# Recording cue sheets

Two scripts, two cue sheets. Each row says: play these paragraphs of the spoken
text, while showing this page at this position.

Paragraph numbers refer to the numbered files:

- `tts-spoken-text-numbered.txt` — the full 15-minute walkthrough, 64 paragraphs
- `tts-site-tour-numbered.txt` — the short site tour, 29 paragraphs

The plain versions without numbers (`tts-spoken-text.txt`,
`tts-site-tour-spoken-text.txt`) are what you feed the TTS engine. The numbered
versions are only for following along while you record.

**How to use them.** Generate the audio first. Play it back, and change the
screen at each paragraph boundary in the table. Every row is a scroll or a
click, never both at once, so nothing has to be timed to the word.

## Notes for both recordings

- **One change per paragraph.** Every row is either a scroll or a click. If a
  row asks for both, do the click while the previous paragraph is still playing.
- **Scroll slowly.** Tables are the content on this site. A fast scroll makes
  them unreadable on a phone.
- **Zoom to 110–125%** so field tables are legible on a small screen.
- **Collapse sidebar groups you are not discussing**, so the tree fits in one
  frame whenever the script talks about the sidebar.
- **The four viewer pauses in the full walkthrough** are at paragraphs 17, 27,
  31 and 34. Hold the screen still and leave two seconds of silence.
- **Hold on the checklist** at paragraph 40 of the full walkthrough for a full
  five seconds. It is the most important screen in that video.
